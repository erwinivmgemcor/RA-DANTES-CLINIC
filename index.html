About Us

Committed to Quality Maternal Care in Norzagaray

R.A. Dantes Lying-In Clinic has been a trusted partner for mothers in Bulacan, offering safe, affordable, and compassionate prenatal, delivery, and postnatal care. Our team of licensed midwives and healthcare professionals ensures that every mother and newborn receives the highest standard of care in a clean and nurturing environment.

Safety First

We follow strict hygiene protocols and emergency preparedness to ensure the safety of every mother and child.

Licensed Staff

Emergency Ready

Our Services

Complete Maternity Care Services

From prenatal check-ups to delivery and postnatal care, we provide comprehensive services for every stage of motherhood.

Prenatal Check-Up & Monitoring

Regular monitoring of mother and baby's health, including weight, blood pressure, and fetal development tracking.

24/7 Normal Spontaneous Delivery

Safe and comfortable birthing experience with 24-hour midwife assistance and emergency support.

Ultrasound Services

TVS (Transvaginal), Pelvic Ultrasound, and BPS (Biophysical Profile) performed by trained professionals.

Postnatal Care & Family Planning

Post-delivery check-ups, breastfeeding support, and family planning counseling for the mother's recovery.

Newborn Screening & Immunization Advice

Essential newborn screening tests and guidance on immunization schedules for your baby's health.
